| 17:33 | `b2734d8` | **Gratbot dance move 2 — actually fixed, and the reason it survived the last fix.** Reported *again*: key 2 sank the robot into the ground nose-first. Two compounding bugs, neither the one `daa6792` addressed. (1) `poseDance` built its rotation from `setFromAxisAngle(_right, …)` / `(_fwd, …)`, but those are **world**-space vectors while the quaternion is **post-multiplied** onto `mesh.quaternion` — and a post-multiply applies its axis in the **body-local** frame. The two coincide only at heading 0, so the "nose-up" pitch **inverted to nose-DOWN at heading π** and degenerated into a **roll at ±π/2**; the previous fix was verified at spawn heading, where it genuinely worked. (2) `mesh.quaternion` is *also* the terrain-tilt smoothing state (`slerp(_tiltQ, 1−e^(−10dt))`), so composing the dance offset into it fed the offset back into its own smoothing — steady-state error ≈ `d/k`, i.e. **framerate-dependent runaway**: nose-up **31.5° intended → 54° at dt 0.1, 85° at 0.05, 88.9° at 0.016 (real 60 fps)**, which buried the whole chassis (feet **0.96 m below** the surface) at *every* heading. **Fixes:** the smoothing state is split out into **`_baseQ`** (`update()` slerps it and copies to `mesh.quaternion`; `poseDance` writes `_baseQ · _danceQ`) so the pose is an **output, never an accumulator**; dance rotations use constant **body-local axes** (`_bodyX/Y/Z`), making them heading-proof; and TWO-LEG is a real rear-up that **seats the body on its hind feet** — under the full pose `q`, the body-local hind-foot line `L` is put back on the ground via `position = P_world − q·L`. That general form matters: deriving the rise from the dance pitch alone (`hindLz·sin θ`) ignores the terrain tilt already in `q` and swung the rear corner **0.23 m into a 20° hillside**. `REAR_PITCH = 0.6` (~38°) stays moderate because the terrain-hug tilt **stacks** on it and rearing swings the rear-bottom chassis corner down *and* backward, toward ground rising behind the pivot. Because hip↔own-foot distance is a rotation invariant (0.68 m vs 0.88 m reach), the hind legs are solvable at any rear angle and their plant never slides (fore/aft **0.2 mm**). The seat shift is recorded in `_danceOfs` and **undone at the top of `update()`** — `mesh.position` IS the step integrator's state, so leaving it applied drifts the walker backwards every frame. A `danceRamp` ease-in stops the rear-up and paw lift-off popping in one frame. **Why the E2E missed it:** it sampled the body **origin** (rises `+0.30` by construction, blind to a nose-dive) at one heading and one `dt` — precisely the two axes the bug varied along. It now sweeps **heading × framerate** over the 7 chassis shell corners and asserts pose invariance + zero drift (2 checks → 6). Verified headless **12/12 flat** (5 moves × 4 dt × 8 headings: min chassis **+0.375 m** AGL, no foot under the surface, pitch spread **0.0000°**, drift **0.0 m**/400 frames) and **8/8 on 20° grades** in 4 directions (min chassis **+0.089 m**).
