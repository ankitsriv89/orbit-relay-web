| 15:35 | `9eb4015` | **Mars Sim — humanoid leg IK rewrite + van speed.** Fixed the long-standing "humanoid has no lower half / legs are over its back" bug, reported from a `van-driving` screen recording. Root cause was **not** a stale deploy (as logged on 2026-07-20 against `a302ba0`) but a genuine defect in `humanoid.js`'s `solveIk`: the hip angle used `atan2(dz,dy) − atan2(b·sin γ, a + b·cos γ)`, and because `FEMUR (0.208) < TIBIA (0.259)` the term `a + b·cos γ` crosses zero as the knee straightens, snapping that offset to ≈**π** and flipping the thigh a full 180°. A standing leg sits at full extension (hip→ground 0.458 vs leg length 0.467), so the flip fired on effectively every idle frame — measured 68.9°→**180.0°** as reach went 0.400→0.467, where the correct angle goes 35.1°→**0.0°**. Two further latent errors in the same solve: angles were computed in **Pelvis-local** space but applied about each **bone's local X** (L_Thigh's bind is a ~180° X-flip, so the applied rotation was mis-signed — brute-force solving against the real bind transforms showed hip sign OPPOSITE in every test pose), and the rig's bind knee is already pre-bent ~0.335 rad (a constant offset the solve ignored). Replaced the whole thing with a **world-space, bind-relative two-bone IK**: solve the femur/tibia *directions* using the correct law-of-cosines `β = acos((r²+a²−b²)/(2ra))` with the knee poled toward body-forward (so it can never hinge backward, with no cross-product handedness to get wrong), then re-aim each bone by the **minimal rotation from its own bind direction** — which preserves bind twist and assumes nothing about how the exporter oriented the local axes. Bone data read directly out of the GLB's glTF JSON chunk to confirm `L_Calf`/`L_Foot` sit at `(0,.208,0)`/`(0,.259,0)` (bones extend along local **+Y**). Added `ANKLE_H = 0.12` so plant points (which are *sole* positions) drive the **ankle** bone without sinking the boots; retired the now-unused `MAX_BEND`. Numeric FK check: solved foot lands within **0.5 mm** of target across idle/step-forward/step-back/crouch/lift, knee forward in all cases. Also bumped **`VAN_SPEED` 2.6 → 5.5 m/s** (~20 km/h) — 2.6 was only ~1.8× the 1.4 m/s walk and *below* the rover's slowest usable gear (G1 = 2.1 m/s), making the van slower than walking; 5.5 is ~4× walking, still under rover G2 (6.3 m/s). Audited `walker-rig.js` for the same defect — **clean**, it already uses the correct `acos((L1²+R²−L2²)/(2·L1·R))` form, so Ongak/Makadane are unaffected.
